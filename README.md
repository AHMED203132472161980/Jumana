​Jumana: The 11-Gate Holistic Life Management & Health Guardian System
​Executive Summary:
"Jumana" is an integrated intelligent system designed to bridge the gap between daily lifestyle management and real-time health monitoring. Based on an 11-gate architecture, the system provides proactive assistance, bio-metric health tracking, and emotional intelligence support, aiming to create a seamless, accessible, and human-centric medical monitoring experience for all global users.
​Key Innovations:
​The 11-Gate Architecture: A hierarchical structure covering productivity, health, entertainment, and professional medical liaison.
​Proactive Health Protocol: A proprietary 15-minute relaxation-pre-measurement protocol that ensures data accuracy before biometric health scans.
​Continuous Medical Liaison: Real-time generation of health reports, synchronized with medical history.
​Adaptive AI Support: A system that respects the user's workflow and uses emotional intelligence to offer encouragement or corrective suggestions.
